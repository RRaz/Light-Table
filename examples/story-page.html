Colours

Eight slides · a test gallery

These eight colour slides are not photographs. They are the test gallery that ships with the light table, and they exist for one reason: to make it obvious when something works and when it does not. A colour that is wrong is easier to spot than a photograph that is merely a little soft.

Use them to check a new installation before your own work goes in — the dial, the thumbnail strip, the magnifier and the slide show all behave the same as they will with real photographs.
