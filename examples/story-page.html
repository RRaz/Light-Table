Colours

Eight slides · a rainbow in working order

Red, orange, yellow, green, teal, blue, indigo, violet — the whole way round, in the order the eye expects. No photographs here. Just colour, doing what colour does, so that anything out of place is obvious at a glance.

Run the dial along the ring, open the magnifier, start the slide show. Every control behaves exactly as it will with your own work, and a wrong red is much easier to argue with than a photograph that is only slightly soft.
